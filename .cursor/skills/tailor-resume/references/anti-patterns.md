# Anti-patterns

Never:

- Invent employers, titles, dates, or tools
- Add skills from JD that are not in `resume.md`
- Inflate seniority (e.g. "led" without leadership evidence)
- Hide keywords in white text or metadata
- Paste JD paragraphs into the resume body
- Add unverifiable metrics
- Add anecdotes, opinions, or humor during the Humanizer pass
- Turn `tailored-resume.md` into a first-person essay
- Invent company knowledge in `cover-letter.md` beyond the posted JD
- Write a negative or self-rejecting cover letter (gap catalogs, "I am not the candidate", not-X-but-Y)
- State negatives on `tailored-resume.md` ("I don't do this", "not a pre-sales role", "does not include Java")
- Omit roles, bullets, skills, certs, awards, languages, or education that are in `resume.md`
- Write terse, trite, or half-phrase bullets (noun stacks, dash chains, keyword soup)
