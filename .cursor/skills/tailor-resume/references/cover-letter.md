# Cover letter

Write `jobs/<company-slug>/<role-slug>/cover-letter.md` on every tailor run.

## Fact contract

Only `resume.md` and the posted JD (including scraped `jd.md`). Unsupported JD asks stay out of the letter; they belong in the fit report **Do not add** section. Do not invent skills to sound more qualified.

## Tone (required)

The letter is a **yes**: confident, warm, and forward-looking. The reader should finish wanting to interview, not wondering why you applied.

- Lead with the role and **why you are a strong fit**, using 2–3 concrete proof points from `resume.md`.
- Write as someone who wants the job. Show energy for the team’s problem (from the JD) without sales fluff or fake company intimacy.
- Put **gaps only in `fit-report.md`**. Do not use the cover letter to disqualify yourself, list missing stack items, or argue against the posting.
- Forbidden shapes: "I am not that profile", "If the bar is firm, I am not the candidate", "This is not a match", "It is not [their job]", "I have not done X", "I would not present Y as a strength", "the closest I have", "wanted to be direct" as a warning.
- Do not use not-X-but-Y contrasts ("That is people leadership, not field work").
- Location: if `resume.md` shows a different city, one positive line is enough (for example interested in the posted location). Do not dwell on visa, sponsorship, or relocation ineligibility.
- Close with a clear ask to talk and the email from `resume.md`.

## Shape

```markdown
# Cover letter — <Role> at <Company>

<Date if known from the run; otherwise omit>

Dear Hiring Manager,

<paragraphs>

Sincerely,
Abhishek Das
```

Use a named greeting only when the JD names a person. Otherwise `Dear Hiring Manager,` is enough.

## Length and content

- 3–4 short paragraphs (about 250–400 words).
- Open with the role and one concrete reason the fit is real (from `resume.md`, aligned to the JD).
- Then 2–3 proof points: teams led, shipped work, metrics. Mirror JD language only when it is factually equivalent.
- Close with interest in a conversation and how to reach you (email from `resume.md`).
- No "I am writing to apply", "I hope this email finds you well", or "passionate about synergies".
- After drafting, apply `prose-clarity.md` (Humanizer, first-person voice). Keep the letter positive through Humanizer; do not reintroduce gap-cataloguing.
