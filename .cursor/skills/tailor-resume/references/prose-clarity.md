# Prose clarity + Humanizer

After the first tailored draft exists, expand cryptic phrasing, then run Humanizer. Do not skip this pass on `tailored-resume.md` or `cover-letter.md`. Do not Humanize `fit-report.md`.

## Always

1. Read `.cursor/skills/humanizer/SKILL.md`.
2. Run it in **embedded / file mode**: write only the final text to the file. Do not dump mark-tells or intermediate drafts in chat.
3. Do not invent names, numbers, tools, employers, or claims. If a sentence needs a detail you do not have, simplify it.
4. Keep JD-aligned keywords and every metric from the draft that traces to `resume.md`.
5. Humanizer may shorten dull padding. **Do not shorten below comprehension.** Telegram fragments are the problem this pass exists to fix.

## Tailored resume (constrained)

Voice: technical and factual. Neutral and plain. No first-person "I". No opinions, humor, or essay asides. No negatives about what the candidate does not do.

- Keep the heading and bullet structure in `markdown-resume-structure.md`. Do not convert the resume into narrative paragraphs.
- Expand cryptic fragments into **full sentences**: who or what acted, the object, and the outcome. Prefer "Led the Risk Assess launch as a high-availability full-stack app." over stacked nouns and dash chains.
- One idea per bullet; one or two full sentences. Summary: three to four complete sentences, not keyword soup or half phrases.
- Do not drop facts, jobs, skills, or education from `resume.md` to "sound human" or to shorten the page.
- Keep date-range punctuation in headings (for example `June 2023–Present`). Elsewhere, follow Humanizer on em dashes and en dashes used as clause glue.
- Do not restyle section titles into sentence case if that would break ATS parsing of standard labels (Summary, Work Experience, Skills, Education).

## Cover letter (full Humanizer)

Voice: first person, specific, professional, and **positive**. Follow `cover-letter.md`.

- Three to four short paragraphs.
- No chatbot openers, no sales inflation, no invented company knowledge beyond the posted JD.
- Facts only from `resume.md` plus the posted JD.
- Keep salutations and sign-offs (Humanizer: letters predate chatbots).
- Do not "humanize" into a confession of gaps. Humanizer's not-X-but-Y rule applies: never close a proof point by saying what the job is not.
