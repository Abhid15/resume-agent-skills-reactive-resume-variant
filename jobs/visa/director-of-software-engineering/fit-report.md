## Overall fit

**Tier:** Moderate  
**Verdict:** Reasonable fit with gaps - review gaps before applying

Twelve-plus years, current management of 11+ engineers, and a strong GenAI-in-SDLC track record map well to Visa’s ask for technical leadership, distributed systems, and generative AI productivity. Gaps are real: the posting is Director-level over Loyalty / Customer Engagement platforms, prefers Java/Spring and Angular/TypeScript, and centers on real-time payments-adjacent transaction systems—none of which are evidenced as domain or stack depth in `resume.md`. Apply only if you can speak honestly to Manager→Director scope and the payments/Loyalty domain gap; position the resume around engineering leadership, cloud/distributed systems, observability, and GenAI tooling—not Loyalty or Java.
